## Launch recommendation

**Superola V1 — Marketplace Leads**

Marketplace discovery → provider profile → customer verification → one-recipient direct RFQ → provider clarify/quote/decline → asynchronous in-platform text → customer-reported outcome.

The proposed V1 ends at a customer-reported proceed/not-proceeding/unknown outcome with provenance where measurable. Booking, payment, payout, service completion, and review are later states. General request broadcasting is deferred until routing consent, eligibility, fan-out, deduplication, abuse, response-window, closure, rerouting, and liquidity evidence exist.
