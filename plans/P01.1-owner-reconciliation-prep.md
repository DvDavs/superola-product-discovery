### Critical review findings reconciled

| Finding | Severity | Resolution |
|---|---|---|
| The CORE question set could invite the owner to ratify the V1 slice implicitly by answering only inside its frame. | HIGH | Added an explicit challenge invitation to the meeting opening and to CORE Q2 so rejecting the proposed boundary is a first-class answer. |
| A meeting guide that records answers without provenance would let a verbal statement become an evidence-labelled requirement. | HIGH | The capture template records source, date, and speaker, and routes every answer through the matrix at `OWNER_MATERIAL` or `OWNER_INTERVIEW` provenance before any canonical document changes. |
| A pass/fail gate on every uncertainty would block P02/P04 permanently. | MEDIUM | The gate distinguishes BLOCKING from CAN REMAIN OPEN and accepts bounded scenarios for geography/language. |
| Requesting a legacy export at the meeting would create a privacy exposure before controls exist. | MEDIUM | The checklist stages legacy schema/export to a later authorized, controlled request and forbids credentials or production exports over informal channels. |
| The six-month topic could be read as a commitment simply by appearing in the question list. | MEDIUM | It is framed only as "commitment or planning hypothesis?" and the preview still contains no timeline. |
| Gate G-07 required an owner decision on customer verification timing, contradicting `docs/05-roadmap/mvp-scope.md`, which charters P04 to compare pre-submit, just-in-time, and guest-then-verify. | HIGH | G-07 now blocks only on the identity boundary — whether a request may reach a provider from an unidentified customer. Timing moved to CAN REMAIN OPEN with the mvp-scope reference. |
| Gate G-06 was BLOCKING while its only meeting question sat in IF TIME, so a short meeting could silently leave a blocking item unasked. | MEDIUM | G-06 and the OR-010 ledger row now state the promotion condition and the follow-up obligation explicitly. |
| The reconciliation ledger referenced meeting questions as "CORE Q1–Q7" while the guide labels them C1–C7 and T1–T5. | LOW | Labels aligned to C/T across both documents. |
