# Build vs Buy — Evaluation Framework

> Classifications are `PROPOSED` heuristics, not decisions.

## Likely core / build candidates

Marketplace-specific listing/profile behavior, service taxonomy, geography/service-area rules, requests, quotes, ranking, promotion allocation, and marketplace workflows may create differentiating value and require domain ownership.

## Strong buy-or-managed candidates

Payment processing, subscription billing, transactional email, SMS, push transport, maps/geocoding, media delivery/transcoding, CAPTCHA/bot protection, identity providers, analytics infrastructure, and AI models are commodity categories worth evaluating as managed services.

## Required decision record

For each capability compare:

- strategic differentiation and required control;
- product fit and customization limits;
- implementation time and opportunity cost;
- fixed, variable, and scale-dependent pricing;
- operating, reliability, security, compliance, and support burden;
- data access, portability, contract/terms risk, lock-in, and exit cost;
- integration failure modes and fallback/degradation strategy;
- the measurable trigger to reconsider build vs buy.

Low monthly hosting cost is not the same as low total cost of ownership.
