## 1. Why this document exists

P03 produced seven `PROPOSED` ADRs in one act. Reviewing them together surfaced three packaging defects that are not disagreements with P03's research:

1. **`ADR-013` bundled decisions with radically different reversal costs.** Platform (VERY HIGH, effectively a rewrite) was packaged with rendering (LOW–MEDIUM, per-surface, and dependent on P04 interaction requirements that do not exist). **One signature would have settled both.**
2. **`ADR-019` bundled an architecture decision, a vendor selection blocked on unresolved legal readings, and a product/UX conclusion.** They sit at three different decision levels and have three different owners.
3. **`ADR-017` was recommended on an axis P03 recorded as unverified.** P03.1 verified it, and **the claim was falsified for two of four vendors.**

Nothing else was reopened. **PostgreSQL, the outbox, hosting, media, email, search, queues and the AI recommendation were not re-evaluated**, because no concrete material defect was found in them.

## 3. Why `ADR-013` stays `PROPOSED` despite `RECOMMEND ACCEPT`

**Repository governance does not permit reading the current David authorization as approval of the result, and this was checked rather than assumed.**

- `docs/02-architecture/adr/README.md` defines `ACCEPTED` as *"approved by the appropriate human decision owner."*
- The only precedent is `ADR-004`, moved to `ACCEPTED` on **an explicit `DAVID_DIRECTIVE` that named the decision.** The index records it as *"the first accepted ADR in the repository"* and states that **every other ADR remains `PROPOSED`.**
- `SRC-014` authorized P03 **to evaluate** `D-01`–`D-15`. Every ADR P03 produced carries *"Approval still required: David"* in its own text.
- `AGENTS.md`: *"Never promote `PROPOSED` to a requirement or accepted decision without recording approval and rationale."*

**Explicit individual approval is therefore required.** `ADR-013` goes into the approval packet with a `RECOMMEND ACCEPT` disposition and **not** with a changed status. **Treating a phase authorization as a decision approval is precisely the accidental-blanket-approval failure this reconciliation exists to prevent.**

## 6. What this reconciliation did NOT do

- It did not re-evaluate PostgreSQL, Spring, hosting, media, email, search, queues, observability, or the AI recommendation. **No concrete material defect was found in any of them**, and reopening a sound decision costs more than it returns.
- It did not change any cost arithmetic. **The three scenario totals are identical to those committed in P03** — USD $75.98, USD $192.99, USD $560.79.
- It did not approve anything.
- It did not reverse P03's AI recommendation. **No AI infrastructure in the V1 critical path, no vector store, no semantic-search dependency, no per-search model cost.** What it added is a bounded, optional, capped **P04 experiment** — AI-assisted request intake / form prefill — recorded in `ai-evaluation.md` §8. That is a P04 decision about UX, not a reversal of a P03 technology finding.
