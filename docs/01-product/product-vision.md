# Product Vision — Internal Discovery Draft

> Status: `PROPOSED`. This is an internal canonical draft, not a completed employer-facing artifact.

Superola aims to become a trusted two-sided marketplace where people planning events can discover, evaluate, contact, and eventually transact with relevant service providers, while providers can present their businesses, respond to qualified demand, manage marketplace capabilities, and earn visibility transparently.

## Intended outcomes

- Customers find providers that fit service, geography, date, and event needs with less uncertainty.
- Providers receive qualified marketplace opportunities and can manage trustworthy profiles.
- Superola retains enough relationship context to improve safety, measurement, service quality, and future transactions.
- Organic discovery and paid promotion coexist without compromising transparency or relevance.
- The product can expand across languages, geographies, web, and future mobile channels incrementally.

## Boundaries still requiring validation

MVP transactions, booking/payment, mobile timing, availability semantics, profile ownership, migration depth, plan/pricing structure, target launch markets, moderation, verification, and review mechanics are unresolved. See the assumption and open-question registers.
