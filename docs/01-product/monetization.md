# Monetization — Evidence Baseline

Owner concepts below have provenance `OWNER_INTERVIEW`; discovery models use `TECHNICAL_DISCOVERY`; project scope treatment uses `DAVID_DIRECTIVE`.

## Provider subscription

- `CONFIRMED (owner hypothesis, unvalidated)`: recurring provider subscription with a relatively small monthly fee.
- `CONFIRMED (illustrative, not approved)`: approximately USD 40/month and a possible three-month free period were discussed.
- `PROPOSED`: explicit plans, subscriptions, trials, entitlements, upgrades/downgrades, cancellation, and renewal should be evaluated as a coherent capability.

## Sponsored placement

- `CONFIRMED (owner hypothesis, unvalidated)`: paid visibility based on category + geography, potentially around ten positions with rotation.
- `CONFIRMED (illustrative, not approved)`: approximately USD 100/month was discussed.
- `PROPOSED`: sponsored campaigns should remain separate from organic ranking and may need targeting, eligibility, time windows, allocation/rotation, disclosure, impressions, clicks, and conversion measurement.

## Local reinvestment

- `CONFIRMED (business strategy idea)`: reinvest part of local revenue in local advertising.
- `CONFIRMED`; provenance `DAVID_DIRECTIVE`: P00/P00.1 treats this as a business strategy idea, not a current software requirement.

## Required discovery

Willingness to pay, packaging, free-tier/trial rules, billing geography/currency/tax, plan ownership, inventory scarcity, seller fairness, disclosure policy, refund/cancellation, unit economics, and the marketplace behaviors monetization may incentivize.
