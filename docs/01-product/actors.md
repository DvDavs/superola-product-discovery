# Actors — Discovery Baseline

`CONFIRMED` actor existence is provenance `OWNER_INTERVIEW`; stated goals and boundaries are `TECHNICAL_DISCOVERY` working interpretations until P01 validates them.

| Actor | Evidence | Known goal | Unresolved boundaries |
|---|---|---|---|
| Customer / event planner | `CONFIRMED` | Find and evaluate suitable event services, communicate, request pricing, and potentially book. | Individual vs organization, guest accounts, team planning, payment role. |
| Provider / vendor | `CONFIRMED` | Present services, geography, availability, media, and respond to demand. | Individual vs business, multi-listing, staff, ownership, service-area rules. |
| Provider staff/member | `PROPOSED` | Help manage a business, listings, availability, conversations, and quotes. | Roles, permissions, auditability, billing ownership. |
| Platform administrator | `PROPOSED` | Govern taxonomy, trust, moderation, accounts, promotions, and operations. | Admin roles, support workflows, approval powers. |
| Superola owner/business operator | `CONFIRMED` stakeholder | Set product/business direction and validate monetization and operating policy. | Formal decision cadence and delegated authority. |
| External service/system | `PROPOSED` supporting actor | Deliver commodity capabilities such as payment, notifications, maps, media, identity, or analytics. | Build-vs-buy decisions not made. |

Actors are not authorization roles or final domain entities. P01 must validate their goals, relationships, and highest-risk workflows.
