## 2026-08-11 — P03 technology and cost evaluation completed

- Produced **one** recommended technology architecture, not several: a single deployable JVM/Spring server-rendered application with build-failing module-boundary enforcement, one PostgreSQL store with schema-per-module, geospatial capability confined to a single predicate, a transactional-outbox job table in that same store, a managed platform in one region, application-owned authentication, zero-egress media storage with audio and video linked rather than hosted, and a governed internal `Place` list with geocoding restricted to provider base addresses.
- Added `docs/03-technology/technology-evaluation.md`, rewrote `cost-model.md`, `infrastructure-options.md`, `technology-radar.md` and `build-vs-buy.md`, and added `ai-evaluation.md`.
- Added `ADR-013`–`ADR-019`, all `PROPOSED — DAVID APPROVAL REQUIRED`. Recorded four decisions deliberately **not** written as ADRs.
- **Moved `ADR-004` from `PROPOSED` to `ACCEPTED`** on an explicit `DAVID_DIRECTIVE` — the first accepted ADR in the repository. `Q-028` and `Q-026` are resolved. The availability directive reaffirms `ADR-005` and `DB-10` but **does not** satisfy `G-06`, which asks what "available" promises a customer rather than what V1 builds; `Q-007` remains open and still blocks P04.
- **Corrected `system-architecture.md` §4.** The rule that the notification delivery intent is created *after* the business transaction commits — *"never in the same transaction, ever"* — is correct for a broker and wrong for a same-store job table, where it opens a silent lost-notification window producing **neither** a delivery-failure state nor a non-response state. The prohibition is re-scoped from the intent to the external call. Two matching lines in `domain-map.md` corrected. `R-044`.
- **Two further P02 edits were attempted and then withdrawn on adversarial review as unjustified overreach** — a narrowing of `system-architecture.md` §8's observability row and a softening of `domain-map.md`'s absolute Analytics read prohibition. Neither had a technology constraint behind it, and the second rested on a false premise: the funnel is answered by SQL against the transactional store, not by Analytics reads. Both are recorded as findings instead of applied as changes.
- **Established that human cost is 88–96% of total cost of ownership at every modelled scenario** — $75.98/month technology against ~$578/month people at Pilot; $560.79 against ~$14,298 at Growth. Recorded the consequence: any decision saving $30 on the invoice and adding an hour of monthly labour is a loss.
- **Adversarial review returned FAIL on the first pass and its findings were resolved rather than argued down.** Notably: a stock-versus-flow error inflated the operator-labour line by ~26 hours/month at Growth and moved total cost of ownership from ~$16,900 to ~$14,859; media moderation was priced in one document and missing from the cost model; `ADR-015`'s deciding argument was technically false, because the transactional outbox is precisely how a broker avoids the dual-write problem; two of `ADR-013`'s four "grounds independent of familiarity" were not differentiators and a third was contradicted by `R-036` in the same phase; and the owner-facing preview contained one fabricated figure and disclosed only half its assumption basis. Two P02 edits were **withdrawn** as unjustified overreach. All corrections are recorded in place rather than silently applied.
- Found that **throughput is not a selection criterion**: the entire marketplace peaks under about ten requests per second at the Growth scenario, so runtime cost reduces to the memory floor.
- Found that **three of P02's four geographic predicates are not geospatial problems** — a tree-ancestry test, a string-prefix match, and an ordered read over an already-filtered set — and confined the geospatial dependency to one predicate so reversal is a single-function swap.
- Found that **the governed internal `Place` list is P02's own design, unrecognised**, reducing the geocoding line ~70× against the same vendor, ~132× against the disqualified platform and dissolving the terms conflict that disqualified the largest mapping platform twice — once for forbidding storage of the resolved coordinate, and once because its only permitted persistence mechanism is the vendor identifier the architecture forbids as place identity.
- **Corrected P03's own brief:** the geocoding volume assumption handed to the work packages presupposed vendor autocomplete for customer-side location, a design P02 did not choose, and differed by ~70–132× in cost depending on candidate. Recorded rather than buried.
- Established that **no import-graph tool in any candidate stack can see a cross-module foreign key**, which is the half of `ADR-001` that determines exit cost, and closed it with a database-catalog test. Answered `ADR-001`'s open question: **enforcement cost does not scale with boundary count** — the marginal cost of a boundary is one annotation, so the boundary set does not need revisiting.
- Recommended **not spending** on natural-language intent mapping: the gate is closed, unanswerable during P03, and the corpus that would open it is itself privacy-gated by a decision assigned to nobody.
- Added risks `R-033`–`R-049` and questions `Q-033`–`Q-035`. Notable: passwordless email-only authentication would silently cancel `D-05`'s free cost lever; provider media metadata emits provider-private location to the public projection; `robots.txt` is not enforcement; and "deletion proof" is a lock-in criterion no store can satisfy.
- Added bilingual `presentation/technology-preview-v0.1.md` — the first owner-facing artifact containing cost figures, with the assumption basis stated in both languages in the same section as the table, and naming no vendor.
- Selected **no** payment technology (`G-02` unresolved), added **no** dedicated search engine, message broker, cache component, container orchestration, multi-region topology, general-purpose infrastructure-as-code, or observability platform. Every rejection carries a measurable trigger.
- P01 and the Marketplace Leads recommendation remain unchanged and `PROPOSED — OWNER VALIDATION REQUIRED`; P02 remains `PROPOSED` apart from the corrections above; P04–P07 remain NOT STARTED.
